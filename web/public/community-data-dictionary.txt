COMMUNITY STUDY — DRAFT FIELD GUIDE / 2026-10-05
Collection is not active. Templates are environmental/project records, not participant intake.
No names, exact residential addresses, symptoms, medical records or participant identifiers.

COMMON FIELDS
record_id/event_id: locally unique non-personal record identifier.
project_id: stable project identifier; retain across phases.
project_phase: proposed, permitting, construction, commissioning, operating, expansion, closed, or unknown. Record actual phase for the observation period; split periods crossing phases.
phase_source_url/source_url: retrievable provenance; avoid links exposing private records.
All *_iso8601: ISO8601 dates/timestamps with timezone offset when time is known; do not invent precision. time_zone: IANA zone, e.g. America/Indiana/Indianapolis.
recorded_at_iso8601: record creation time, distinct from measurement/event time.
notes: methods/context only, no personal or identifying data.
Unknown values: blank, not zero. Environmental records must explain missing values in missing_reason.

ENVIRONMENTAL OBSERVATIONS
site_id: non-residential monitoring station code.
area_role: nearby or comparison; not a claim of proven exposure.
geography_public_label: approved coarse area/station label; no household location.
metric: stable prespecified metric code, e.g. pm25_mean, noise_laeq, water_consumption, water_withdrawal. Do not combine different averaging periods.
value: numeric value only, blank for missing.
unit: explicit unit such as ug/m3, dBA, m3. Do not mix concentration, total and flow-rate units.
period_start/end: measurement window; required to interpret averages and totals.
measurement_basis: measured, modeled, reported, or derived; reported means a third-party disclosure not independently measured by the study.
method: sampling/aggregation method and version; water boundary/source or noise weighting as applicable.
instrument_id: equipment code, not a person identifier; blank if not applicable.
calibration_date: latest relevant calibration/check; method notes should identify collocation/correction history.
qa_flag: pending, accepted, suspect, invalid, missing; accepted means checked against the study plan, not regulatory certification.
completeness_percent: valid observations / expected observations * 100 for the period, 0–100; blank if not applicable. Specify denominator and minimum completeness in the protocol.
missing_reason: not measured, unavailable, instrument outage, invalidated, or explicit explanation.

PROJECT EVENTS
event_type: phase_change, generator_test, outage, construction_activity, expansion, incident, mitigation, or other described event.
operating_capacity_mw: actual operating capacity if documented; not announced/buildout capacity; blank if unknown.
verification_status: primary_source, corroborated, or unverified. Do not publish an allegation as verified fact.

BEFORE LAUNCH — ALL CURRENTLY UNCONFIRMED
1. Select pilot, geography and phase; appoint accountable sponsor and community advisory group.
2. Approve primary outcomes, comparison areas, seasonal baseline, follow-up cadence and analysis/power plan.
3. Secure lawful data access and institutional/public-health determination of research oversight and consent requirements.
4. Establish separate secure participant system if needed; approve retention, access, disclosure/suppression and data-use agreements.
5. Finalize quality-assurance plan, instruments, maintenance responsibilities and independent funding/publication terms.
6. Agree agency response/notification procedures, publish protocol, and train collectors before monitoring begins.
7. Review completeness, protocol deviations and missingness on a prespecified schedule; report uncertainty and null results.
